# Almanac Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Almanac is a personal daily-brief app for Android, built and used by its
author. It is not distributed through an app store and has no user accounts,
servers, analytics, or advertising.

## What the app accesses

- **Calendar.** With your permission, Almanac reads events from the calendars
  on your device to show today's and tomorrow's schedule. It does not create,
  change, or delete calendar events, and calendar data never leaves the device.
- **Notifications.** Almanac schedules one local reminder each morning. No
  notification content is sent to any server.
- **Google Tasks.** If you choose to connect a Google account, Almanac uses the
  Google Tasks API to read and write tasks in your own task lists so they stay
  in sync with the lists in the app. Almanac requests only the Tasks scope and
  your email address (to show which account is connected).

## Where data is stored

Tasks, lists, and settings are stored on your device. Google sign-in tokens
are stored in the device's secure keystore. Nothing is sent to the app's
author or to any third party. The only outside service the app talks to is
Google, and only when you have connected a Google account.

## Revoking access

You can disconnect Google Tasks inside the app at any time, which deletes the
stored tokens. You can also remove Almanac's access from your Google account
at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Uninstalling the app removes
all locally stored data.

## Google API Services

Almanac's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.
